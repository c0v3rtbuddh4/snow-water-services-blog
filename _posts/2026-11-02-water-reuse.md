Water reuse is an important topic in the planning and management of water services. It involves treating water that has already been used so it can be put to another suitable purpose.

## Why does water reuse matter?

Water reuse can help make better use of available water resources. However, the suitability of a reuse scheme depends on the intended application, treatment requirements, environmental considerations and community expectations.

## What should we consider?

* What will the treated water be used for?
* What treatment and monitoring are required?
* What are the energy, cost and environmental implications?
* How will risks be managed and communicated?

## Following the flow

Water reuse is not a one-size-fits-all solution. Its benefits and trade-offs need to be assessed in the context of the wider water system.
