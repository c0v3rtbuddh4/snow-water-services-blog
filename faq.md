<div class="principles faq-principles">
  <div class="principle"><span class="num">01</span><h2>What is SNOW?</h2><p>A visual journal focused on water services, infrastructure, people, sustainability and justice.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span class="num">02</span><h2>Who is it for?</h2><p>Anyone curious about how water services are designed, delivered, governed and experienced.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span class="num">03</span><h2>Can I suggest a post?</h2><p>Yes. Use the contact link on the About page to suggest a topic or share feedback.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span class="num">04</span><h2>How do new articles get published?</h2><p>Each article is a Markdown file in the <code>_posts</code> folder. Once you commit it to GitHub, Pages rebuilds the site and adds it to the journal.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span class="num">05</span><h2>Can I subscribe by email?</h2><p>Not automatically with GitHub Pages alone. You can later connect a mailing-list service if you want to collect subscriptions.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span class="num">06</span><h2>Does SNOW provide professional advice?</h2><p>Articles are for general information and discussion. Readers should check original sources and seek suitable professional advice for decisions that require it.</p></div>
