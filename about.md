  <h2>A name with a story.</h2>
  <p>SNOW is inspired by Dr John Snow, whose work helped reveal how mapping and evidence can uncover the systems behind public health.</p>
  <h2>Why water?</h2>
  <p>Water services are more than pipes, treatment plants and taps. They connect infrastructure, ecosystems, public health, economics, governance and the everyday lives of communities.</p>
  <p>SNOW is a space for evidence-led questions about how water services are designed, delivered, governed and experienced, and how they can become fairer and more resilient.</p>
  <h2>What you'll find here</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Explainers about water and wastewater systems.</li>
    <li>Stories about access, affordability, sustainability and justice.</li>
    <li>Evidence-led discussion of infrastructure choices and their consequences.</li>
  </ul>
